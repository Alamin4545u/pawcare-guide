    Get emergency care right away if your pet has:

    • Difficulty breathing, or gums that are blue, gray, or white
    • Collapse, seizures, or inability to stand
    • Heavy bleeding or a deep wound
    • Suspected poisoning or swallowed toxic items
    • A swollen, hard belly with unproductive retching, which can signal bloat in dogs
    • Straining in the litter box with little or no urine, a serious sign in male cats

    Call your nearest emergency veterinary hospital first.
